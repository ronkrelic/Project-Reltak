v0.00a · Early alpha

A high-fantasy adventure RPG, entirely in your browser. No install, no download, no launcher, no account. Open the link and stand in the world.

A world worth waking

The first clearing is only the beginning. Step into an early alpha where the foundations of an adventure are already taking shape.

  • Choose your beginning.
    Character creation with data-driven races and classes.
  • Walk The Clearing.
    Explore a walkable 3D world, with a camera spanning wide isometric views to first person.
  • Let the dice speak.
    Face a goblin in d20-driven combat. Natural-20 critical hits included.
  • Find your bearings.
    A HUD with a fog-of-war minimap and a session log keeps the journey in view.
  • Set the atmosphere.
    Four interface themes: Astral Relics, Crown and Ember, Moss and Moon, and Obsidian Grimoire.

Built in the open

The whole game is developed publicly. Watch it grow commit by commit on GitHub. Early alpha now; follow along as new lands take shape.

Beyond the horizon

Coming soon: more enemies, distinct class kits, dungeons, and a dragon named Reltak. These are the roads ahead, not promises of what is playable today.
