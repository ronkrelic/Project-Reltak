# Texture sources and credits

## Included: painted textures

The owner supplied three painted seamless images in the project chat and authorized their use for this texture pass. No third-party license is asserted for these supplied images. Each was resized to 1024 × 1024 and JPEG-encoded for this build:

- ../textures/painted/plaster-timber-wall.jpg — inn interior walls and bedroom partitions.
- ../textures/painted/dark-oak-floorboards.jpg — inn floors, stairs, counter, beams, shutter panels and door lintels.
- ../textures/painted/interior-stone-wall.jpg — hearth base, pillars and mantel.

All three are wired in ../../js/game.js. Surface UVs are scaled in metres; the original paving, exterior architecture, rugs and vendored models are unchanged.
